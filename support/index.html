What Shipkit Pipes is

Shipkit Pipes is a text-conversion utility for iOS and macOS. It encodes and decodes text, computes hashes, converts timestamps, and chains those steps into a pipeline.

It runs entirely on the device and needs no network connection.

Requirements

  • iOS 17.0 or later
  • macOS 14.0 or later

Getting help

Support is through this project’s issue tracker on GitHub. Open an issue at github.com/indiagrams/ios-macos-smoketest/issues and describe what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. The issue tracker is the support channel for this app; there is no other queue, so an issue is read rather than triaged away.

Please include your platform and OS version. Do not paste anything confidential into an issue — the tracker is public, and the app never needs your data to reproduce a conversion bug.

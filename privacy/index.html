Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026.

This policy applies to Shipkit Pipes on iOS and macOS. It is published by Indiagram LLC.

What the app collects

Shipkit Pipes collects no data.

The app makes no network requests, and carries no networking entitlement on either platform.

Nothing you type, no result the app produces and no pipeline you build is written to disk, to a log, or off the device.

What the app stores on your device

The app stores five interface settings in its own preferences on your device: which screen was open last, the Format and Direction selections on Encode/decode, and the “Read as” and time zone selections on Timestamps.

Those settings are read and written through UserDefaults, which Apple classes as a required-reason API; the privacy manifest declares that one use under reason CA92.1 — access to information only the app itself can read — and declares nothing else.

Accounts, third parties and tracking

There are no accounts and no sign-in.

The app bundles no third-party SDK.

The app does not track you, and declares no tracking domains.

First release.

Three tools in a sidebar - Encode/decode, Hashing and Timestamps - each opening as a one-step
sequence that extends in place. Base64, URL percent-encoding and HTML entities; MD5, SHA-1,
SHA-256 and SHA-512; Unix epoch to ISO 8601 and local time with a time zone picker. One tool's
output can be taken as the next tool's input, intermediate values stay visible, and steps can
be removed or reordered without retyping the sequence. Sandboxed, with no networking
entitlement.
